# NorthStar Contributor Model

NorthStar is a public knowledge platform with **accountable contribution**. Public discovery remains open. Registration is required to submit material, and every public knowledge change remains subject to human review.

| Layer | Policy |
|---|---|
| Registered contributor | May submit resources, evidence, relationships, and intake-derived drafts; may track the resulting review status. |
| Verified contributor | Earns status through accepted-work history and a manual portfolio review. Receives a visible badge, public contribution history, practical intake allowance, and curator tools. |
| Faster review | Verified material may enter a faster lane, but every item remains eligible for sampled human review and no contributor bypasses publication controls. |
| Appeals | Verification and high-impact moderation/account decisions may be appealed through a recorded, human-reviewed workflow. |
| Public knowledge | Basic public discovery is never paywalled. Future paid experiments must add professional workflow, verified organization management, or governed API capacity rather than restrict public knowledge. |

## Integrity Rules

Activity volume, paid status, and AI confidence do not prove contributor trust. Trust is based on reviewed quality, attributable evidence, manual verification where required, transparent rationale, audit history, and human moderation. A status may be suspended when abuse, deception, undisclosed promotion, privacy violations, or repeated low-quality activity are established through the documented review process.

## Staged Value Experiments

NorthStar may later test a professional research workspace, verified organization management, and governed API capacity. Each experiment requires a separate written hypothesis, success metric, privacy assessment, and owner approval before it changes a user-facing offer or any billing behavior.
