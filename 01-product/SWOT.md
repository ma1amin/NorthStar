# NorthStar SWOT

## Strengths

- Clear problem around fragmented discovery
- Open-source positioning
- Community contribution potential
- Relationship-centric architecture
- Bilingual global direction

## Weaknesses

- Cold-start data problem
- Moderation burden
- Relationship quality is difficult to maintain
- Broad scope may create product complexity

## Opportunities

- Community knowledge preservation
- Cross-category resource discovery
- AI-assisted enrichment
- Graph-based exploration
- External data ingestion

## Threats

- Low-quality submissions
- Duplicate data
- Stale resources
- External-source dependency
- Strong existing discovery platforms
- Abuse and spam

These are strategic working hypotheses and should be validated.
