# Competitive Research

## Status

External competitive research has not been performed in this documentation pass. No competitor claims are treated as verified facts.

## Research Scope

Future research should evaluate:
- Resource directories
- Product discovery platforms
- Alternative/comparison platforms
- Open-source curated lists
- Knowledge graph products
- Bookmarking and read-later products
- Developer resource directories

## Required Output

For each competitor:
- Positioning
- Core audience
- Discovery model
- Contribution model
- Data model
- Relationship support
- Search capability
- Business model
- Open-source status
- Key strengths
- Key weaknesses
- NorthStar opportunity

## Rule

Do not copy competitor features without validating whether they support NorthStar's core problem.
