# NorthStar Value Proposition

NorthStar turns scattered digital-resource discoveries into structured, searchable, connected knowledge.

## For Users

Find resources again, understand what they do, compare alternatives, and discover related tools.

## For Contributors

Preserve useful discoveries and improve a shared resource knowledge base.

## For Communities

Move valuable knowledge from temporary message streams into a persistent reference layer.

## Differentiator

The strongest proposed differentiator is the combination of community-curated resources with a first-class relationship graph, rather than a flat directory.
