# NorthStar User Personas

The following personas are derived from the confirmed target/resource context. They are working personas and should be validated through user research.

## Developer

Needs fast discovery of libraries, repositories, APIs, frameworks, and related tools.

## Startup Founder

Needs tools, accelerators, incubators, SaaS products, research resources, and curated stacks.

## Cybersecurity Professional

Needs security tools, repositories, platforms, intelligence resources, and related technologies.

## Designer

Needs design systems, UI/UX resources, assets, inspiration, and related platforms.

## Researcher / Learner

Needs structured access to research, learning, and knowledge resources.

## Community Contributor

Needs a low-friction way to submit, correct, connect, and curate resources.

## Moderator

Needs tools to maintain quality, resolve duplicates, validate relationships, and manage reports.
