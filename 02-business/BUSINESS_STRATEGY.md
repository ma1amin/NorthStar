# NorthStar Business Strategy

## Current State

Business strategy is intentionally undecided.

## Strategic Principle

Do not compromise the public knowledge/discovery mission merely to create early monetization.

## Future Evaluation Criteria

Any business model should be assessed against:
- Open-source integrity
- User trust
- Data ownership
- Sustainability
- Scalability
- Community incentives
- Legal feasibility
- Competitive differentiation

## Open Decision

Define the unique economic model after the product's core value proposition and community behavior are validated.
