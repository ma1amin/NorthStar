# NorthStar Governance

NorthStar uses a maintainer-led, evidence-based governance model. The project owner and designated maintainers are responsible for releases, security response, moderation policy, role administration, and decisions that affect data integrity or contributor safety.

## Decision-Making

Small fixes may be merged after review by a maintainer. Changes to schema, authentication, authorization, moderation, relationship semantics, public API contracts, data retention, or contributor policy require a documented proposal and at least one maintainer approval. When a decision affects the truthfulness of resource or relationship claims, evidence quality takes precedence over feature velocity.

## Community Contributions

Contributors may submit issues, documentation improvements, code, relationship evidence, category/taxonomy proposals, and product feedback. Maintainers will distinguish product discussions from verified data claims. Code contributions follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md); behavioural expectations follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Moderation and Appeals

Human moderation is the final authority for published resources, relationships, edit suggestions, reports, and AI-assisted drafts. Moderation actions are auditable. A contributor may request a review by providing new evidence, but appeals do not guarantee reversal and must not be used to pressure moderators.
