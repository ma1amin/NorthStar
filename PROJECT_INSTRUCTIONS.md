# NorthStar Project Instructions

## Role

Treat NorthStar as a Resource Intelligence Platform, not as a conventional directory, bookmark manager, or link repository.

## Decision Principles

1. Preserve simplicity for users.
2. Treat resources as knowledge entities.
3. Treat relationships as first-class data.
4. Prefer open standards and vendor neutrality.
5. Design for global use from day one.
6. Provide equal English and Arabic experiences.
7. Keep public discovery available without mandatory registration.
8. Do not turn undecided choices into facts.
9. Separate recommendations from confirmed decisions.
10. Optimize for long-term maintainability and scalability.

## Product Principles

- Discovery before administration.
- Knowledge quality before record quantity.
- Community contribution with moderation.
- Search plus exploration, not search alone.
- Relationships should increase the value of every resource.
- AI should assist, not silently become the source of truth.

## Engineering Principles

- Modular architecture.
- Clear domain boundaries.
- API-first thinking.
- Strong validation.
- Security by design.
- Test critical workflows.
- Document architectural decisions.
- Avoid unnecessary vendor lock-in.

## Documentation Rule

Whenever a decision is not confirmed, use `TBD`, `Open Decision`, or `Recommendation`.

Never fabricate:
- credentials
- ownership details beyond the supplied credit
- business model
- license
- deployment provider
- domain
- AI provider
- final graph database
- final user-role model
