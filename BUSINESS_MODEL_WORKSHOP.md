# NorthStar Hybrid Contributor Model — Decision Workbook

## Decision to Make

NorthStar should remain an open discovery layer while making contributions accountable, high-quality, and rewarding. The immediate decision is not a price list. It is the **contribution contract**: who can submit, how trust is earned, what reviewers decide, and what contributors receive in return.

> **Recommended direction:** public browsing; registration required for every contribution; verified contributor status earned through transparent identity/provenance checks and accepted work; no automatic publication; paid value, if introduced later, attached to professional workflow rather than basic knowledge access.

## Proposed Role Matrix

| Capability | Public visitor | Registered contributor | Verified contributor | Moderator | Administrator |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Browse approved resources and relationships | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Capture/paste material and submit proposals | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| View own intake status and review feedback | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Submit source and relationship suggestions | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Receive contributor profile and accepted-work history | No | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Use expedited review lane | No | No | Proposed, with sampling | No | No |
| Review, approve, reject, or request clarification | No | No | No | Yes | Yes |
| Manage roles, policy, and appeals | No | No | No | Limited | Yes |

## Verification Options

| Option | What it proves | Friction | Risk | Recommendation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Accepted-work history | A contributor has repeatedly provided useful, reviewed information. | Low | Can take time to earn. | Required baseline. |
| Email/domain check | A contributor controls an account or organizational domain. | Low | Does not prove expertise. | Optional support signal. |
| Portfolio/manual review | Evidence of professional or community standing is reviewed by a human. | Medium | Requires moderator time. | Initial verified-contributor path. |
| Organization verification | An organization confirms affiliation and a responsible contact. | Medium | Needs clear verification and renewal policy. | Future organization feature, not first release. |

## Proposed Moderation Rules

1. Every material-derived candidate begins **Pending**. Extraction confidence is assistance, not authority.
2. A contributor can submit many candidate suggestions, but each public resource, source, relationship, or edit requires human approval.
3. Verified contributors may receive faster queue handling only after the team defines sampling, suspension, and appeal rules; no approval bypass is permitted.
4. Duplicate confirmation preserves the original record and community references; it cannot destructively merge user history.
5. Promotional, deceptive, copied-without-permission, or privacy-invasive submissions are rejected with a reason and may affect contributor standing.

## Value Exchange Hypotheses

| User group | Free public value | Accountable contribution value | Future professional value to test—not implement yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discoverer | Search, graph navigation, trusted context, collections. | Ability to save and propose improvements. | Advanced personal research workspace. |
| Contributor | Public profile and contribution status. | Feedback, provenance, accepted-work history, verification path. | Priority support or richer curation tools. |
| Curator | Shared public collections. | Attribution and a transparent quality history. | Team workspaces and governed private collections. |
| Organization | Public profile/relationship context when approved. | Correct inaccurate information with evidence. | Administrative verification and controlled API capacity. |

## Anti-Gaming Controls

The initial model should require rate limits, source/provenance context, transparent review rationale, duplicate detection, audit logs, a suspension/appeal path, and manual enforcement. It should not use paid status, unreviewed AI confidence, raw activity volume, or fabricated social proof as a trust signal.

## Approved Workshop Decisions

The owner approved the following decisions on 2026-08-13:

1. **Verification threshold:** accepted-work history plus manual portfolio review is the initial verified-contributor standard.
2. **Expedited handling:** both queues exist. Verified contributors receive a faster lane that remains subject to sampled human review; the standard queue remains human-moderated.
3. **Contributor benefit:** all listed initial benefits are in scope: verified badge, public contribution history, practical intake allowances, and curator tools.
4. **Commercial learning:** all three hypotheses may be evaluated later—professional research workspace, organization verification/management, and governed API capacity—without restricting public knowledge access.
5. **Appeals:** verification and high-impact account/moderation decisions receive an in-product appeal path.

## Recommendation for the First Release

Adopt the proposed role matrix; start verification through accepted-work history plus manual portfolio review; run a standard moderated queue alongside a faster, sampled-review verified lane; provide both recognition and practical contributor benefits; evaluate the three value hypotheses only after quality and retention evidence exists; and permit appeals for verification and high-impact account actions.
