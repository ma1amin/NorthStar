# NorthStar Code of Conduct

NorthStar is committed to a welcoming, professional, and constructive community. Contributors, maintainers, and participants must treat others with respect, avoid harassment and discrimination, credit work appropriately, and challenge ideas rather than people.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, hateful conduct, intimidation, publishing private information, deliberate misinformation, manipulation of community signals, and bad-faith disruption of moderation or contribution processes.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the security or governance channel described in the repository. Maintainers may clarify expectations, request changes, remove content, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the integrity of the knowledge graph.
