# AI Data Pipeline

1. Resource submitted or discovered.
2. Metadata fetched from permitted source.
3. Content normalized.
4. Duplicate detection runs.
5. AI enrichment may run.
6. Suggested fields are presented or queued for review.
7. Approved data becomes canonical.
8. Search index updates.
9. Relationship suggestions are generated.
10. Approved relationships become graph data.

Every AI-generated field should be distinguishable internally from verified or user-supplied data.
